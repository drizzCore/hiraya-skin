Our Story

Skincare built from
a personal need.

Founded 2022 · Manila, PH

She couldn't find skincare made for her.

Hiraya Skin was born from a simple frustration. Our founder, Maria Santos, spent years searching for skincare that actually worked on her morena complexion — products that understood the Philippine heat, the humidity, and the beauty of deeper skin tones.

Every shelf she browsed was filled with formulas designed for cooler climates, lighter skin, or dramatically higher budgets. She decided to stop looking and start building.

Working alongside Filipino dermatologists, Maria developed a line of clean, effective, and affordable skincare — tested specifically on morena and mixed skin, in Manila's real climate. No lab-perfect conditions. No European benchmarks. Just what works, here.

"Hiraya" means "the fruit of one's hope and dreams" in Filipino. It felt right.

Maria Santos, Founder & CEO
